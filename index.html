FAQ

Questions?

Is this actually free?

Yes, completely. No hidden costs, no account required, no credit card. Just submit and we broadcast it.

How long until my ad goes live?

Most ads are reviewed and approved within 1 hour. You'll receive an email when your ad enters the rotation.

What can't I advertise?

Anything that violates Twitch's community guidelines — adult content, hate speech, illegal products, gambling, or misleading claims. We review every submission before it goes live.

How many times will my ad play?

Each submission plays once in the rotation. Submit again to get more airtime.

Can I submit multiple ads?

Yes — just submit the form again with a new ad. Each submission is reviewed independently.
