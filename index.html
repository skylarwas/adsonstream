FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS

How long until my ad goes live?

Most ads are reviewed and approved within 1 hour. You'll receive an email confirmation when your ad enters the rotation.

What content is not allowed?

Anything that violates Twitch's Terms of Service — including adult content, hate speech, weapons, gambling, or misleading claims. All submissions are reviewed by AI and a human moderator.

Can I run my ad multiple times?

Yes. Each submission is one play. Submit the same ad as many times as you want — each gets its own slot in the queue.

How many viewers will see my ad?

Viewership varies. The stream runs 24/7, so your ad will reach whoever is tuned in at that moment. We don't guarantee a minimum viewer count.

Whats the point of this?

This is just a fun project that I've been working on for a while.

What happens after my ad plays?

It plays once and is removed from the rotation. If you want it to run again, just submit it again for free.
